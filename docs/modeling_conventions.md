# Modeling Conventions and Scope

This project is a **connectome-constrained controller** built from a navigation-related subset of the public MaleCNS connectome. It is not a whole-brain or fully biophysical simulation of *Drosophila*.

## Circuit scope

The final recurrent controller contains 532 neurons drawn from the following families:

- FB5AB
- PFNa
- PFNp
- PFNm
- hDeltaC
- FC2B
- FC2C
- FB5N
- PFL2
- PFL3

After filtering weak recurrent connections, 5,274 edges were retained.

## Neurotransmitter sign convention

For the current model, acetylcholine is treated as excitatory and glutamate as inhibitory. This is a simplified modeling convention and does not represent full receptor-specific synaptic physiology.

## Recurrent scaling

The recurrent weight matrix is spectrally rescaled to a target spectral radius of 0.9 for stable recurrent dynamics. This scaling is a numerical modeling choice rather than a biological measurement.

## Bilateral odor mapping

Left and right odor receptor responses are mapped virtually to the FB5AB pair. This should not be interpreted as experimentally established direct antenna-to-FB5AB anatomy.

## PFN encoding

PFN phase information and wind-side tuning are partly inferred or model-defined. Body heading is supplied externally to the sensory encoder rather than being generated by a reconstructed internal heading-compass circuit.

## PFL motor readout

The final motor readout uses 36 PFL2/PFL3 neurons and a frozen nonlinear MLP. The decoder was trained against a designed local plume-tracking policy. Therefore, the motor mapping is learned and should not be interpreted as a direct reconstruction of biological premotor circuitry.

## Odor plume

The environment uses a stochastic intermittent filament plume model. It is not a Navier–Stokes computational-fluid-dynamics simulation.

## Navigation benchmark

The final random benchmark samples randomized starts and odor sources with an initial separation of roughly 9–14 arena units. Starts are generated approximately downwind of the odor source. Therefore, the benchmark evaluates odor-source localization from randomized downwind conditions rather than arbitrary waypoint navigation.

The odor-source coordinates are used only for evaluation and stopping. They are not supplied to the controller.

## Perturbation and rewiring analyses

Family perturbation and degree-preserving rewiring results are model-level causal or structural sensitivity analyses. They do not demonstrate that the same effects necessarily occur in vivo.

## Claims that should be avoided

The project does not demonstrate:

- a complete *Drosophila* brain or CNS simulation
- exact receptor-level neurotransmitter dynamics
- experimentally established antenna-to-FB5AB wiring
- a reconstructed E-PG heading system
- biologically proven PFN phase tuning
- a biologically derived PFL motor decoder
- arbitrary target navigation
- full fluid-dynamic odor transport
- proof that model perturbation effects occur identically in living flies
